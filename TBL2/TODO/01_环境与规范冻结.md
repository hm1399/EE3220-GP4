## 建议冻结的规范

- `UART_BASE = 0x4000_0000`
- `TIMER_BASE = 0x4000_0010`
- `UART_TXDATA` 写字符
- `UART_STATUS[0] = tx_ready`
- `TIMER_STATUS[0] = tick_pending`
- 向 `TIMER_STATUS` 写 1 清除 tick
- SoC 时钟按顶层参数走，不在 timer 里写死
